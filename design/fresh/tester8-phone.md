# Tester 8: first-time player, normal (non-folding) iPhone, paid £1.99

Judged only from still frames, viewed in order: 01, 03-08 (with 07b), 09, 10, 12, 13, 13b, 14, 15, 16, 20, 02, 23, 24, 40-47, and the app icon.

## 1. Goal?
You steal loot from a sleeping treasure-chest monster (a mimic) without waking it. You open it a little, drag the coins and gems into your bag, then bank them before it wakes up. A run ends when your candle burns out, and your score is the gold you banked. Clear from the home tagline ("Don't wake the mimic") and the six-step First Chest tutorial.

## 2. Controls on my phone?
- **Lever on the right of the mimic panel.** Slide it up to open the lid and slide it down to bank (03, 04, 07, 08).
- **Dragging loot.** Drag coins and gems from the chest interior into the bag in the middle (05, 10).
- **Stopping.** When it's stirring you freeze, which means stop moving (06, 12).
- **Pause.** A gold pause button sits top right.

The home screen says "Drag its lid up... slowly." (01, 02, 44), but in the game you never drag the lid. You slide a lever, so the home hint doesn't match the controls.

## 3. Meters and labels
- **NOISE bar.** Fills green, then orange, then red, and ends in a set of teeth. If it fills up, it bites.
- **Candle on the left.** This is your time. It drops when you get bitten ("-10s" in 13), and a clock shows the time left near the end ("0:15" in 16).
- **IN BAG.** Loot you're carrying, marked "not safe yet".
- **BANKED.** Safe gold.
- **Name plate.** The mimic's name and type, e.g. "Dozy Doris, Heavy sleeper, Wooden" or "Mumbling Milo, Dreamer, Royal".
- **GOLD x2 / x3 coin.** Shown on gilded and royal chests.
- **HUSH CHAIN GOLD x1.x badge.** Sits next to the noise bar.
- **Results tiles.** Banked chests, Hushed while it slept, Bites.
- **Rank bar.** "Pickpocket Rank 1, Next: Cutpurse".

## 4. Why does it bite, and what does it cost?
It bites when the noise gets too loud: moving too fast, or not freezing while it's stirring. The open eye in 12 and 13 is the warning. A bite ("CHOMP! Too loud!" in 13) takes everything in your bag (-179) and 10 seconds off the candle. That's harsh but fair, and the stakes are easy to read.

## 5. How do you keep loot?
Slide the lever back down to bank it. That moves IN BAG into BANKED ("Banked! Gently does it" in 07b, "BANKED +975" in 16). If you bank while it's still asleep you build a Hush Chain, which multiplies your gold ("Hush Chain! Sleepy banks pay more" in 09 and 14).

## 6. Does it feel like a complete, polished paid game?
Mostly yes. There's a real tutorial, named mimics with personalities, chest tiers (wooden, gilded, royal), relics, a 24-item codex, 12 badges, a Daily Dungeon, Game Center, ranks and a proper settings screen. The art is consistent and charming, and the results screen is satisfying.

What still feels unfinished or cheap on a normal phone:
- **Dead space at the top.** The top ~17% of the screen is a flat wood panel with nothing on it, in every gameplay frame. On a single screen the layout looks split in two, and the top half is mostly the mimic face with big empty margins.
- **Black, empty chest interior.** Between chests (09, 14, 16) the interior goes black and empty, which looks like loading.
- **Same frame twice.** 13 and 13b look identical, so the "creak" moment isn't visible as anything different.

## 7. Confusing, unreadable, misaligned, cut off or ugly
- **03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 07b: tutorial text clipped.** The first letter of every tutorial line is cut off or jammed against the left edge of the scroll ("Slide", "Drag", "It's", "Banked").
- **07b, 09, 10, 14, 15: Hush Chain badge cut off.** The "HUSH CHAIN" text runs off the right edge of the badge and touches the frame.
- **07b: numbers don't agree.**
  - The big text says "BANKED +119" but the BANKED counter shows 115.
  - The big text says "Hush Chain x1.1" but the badge says x1.2.
- **07: ruby covers a label.** A flying ruby covers the "IN BAG" label, which reads "IN E".
- **09, 14: banner crammed.** "Hush Chain! Sleepy banks pay more" is jammed into its banner, with the end of "more" touching the end cap and tucked under the pause button.
- **15: two messages at once.** "Drag the loot into your bag" stays up while the Relic Found card is showing, and the card overlaps the side rails.
- **16: loot covers the HUD.** Banked loot flies over the noise bar and the BANKED label.
- **41, 45: chevron covers numbers.** With bigger text, the scroll chevron sits on top of "+363" and "+358", and the chest row is squeezed against the Home/Again buttons.
- **42, 46: relic names truncated.** "The Bottomless G..." and "Sir Rattlesworth'...".
- **44: Daily Dungeon status truncated.** It reads "Today's try...".
- **40: badge icon unclear.** The Relic Hunter icon is an unreadable grey blob.
- **01, 02, 44: wrong hint.** "Drag its lid up" contradicts the lever control.
- **07, 20, 44: Dynamic Island in the capture.** It shows as a black pill on some frames only, which is inconsistent.

## 8. Would the icon make me tap it?
Yes. A chunky cartoon chest with fangs and a glowing eye peeking out, plus scattered coins, reads instantly as "mimic, loot, danger" and has character. The coin at the bottom right is close to the corner and may get clipped by the iOS icon mask.

## 9. Scores (/10)
| | Score |
|---|---|
| Understood | 8 |
| Want to play | 7 |
| Looks finished / premium | 6.5 |

**Verdict:** The concept and the risk/reward loop are clear, and there's plenty of content for the price. What makes it feel less finished are the clipped tutorial text, the cut-off Hush Chain badge, the mismatched numbers, and the empty top panel and black interior on a normal phone.

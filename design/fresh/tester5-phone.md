# Tester 5: first-time player on a normal (non-folding) iPhone, judging from stills only

## 1. What's the goal?
Open sleeping mimic chests, grab the coins, gems and relics inside, then shut the lid to bank them before the mimic wakes up or the candle burns out. Each run is a string of chests (9 in 20-results-mid). Your score is the gold you bank. You also rank up (Pickpocket, Cutpurse, Lockpick, Cat Burglar), fill a relic codex (24) and earn badges (12). It's clear by the end of the tutorial card (08).

## 2. Controls on my phone
- A brass lever on the right edge of the top half. Slide it up slowly to raise the lid, slide it down to shut and bank.
- Drag loot from the chest interior (bottom half) into the bag in the middle of the HUD.
- A pause button at top right of the bottom half.
On a normal phone it's a one-thumb lever plus drag. Nothing asks me to fold anything, which is good. The lever is small and tucked hard against the right edge, though, and in 07 and 08 its knob slides under the pink tongue/lip and is half hidden (at the very bottom in 08).

## 3. Meters and labels
- **Candle** (left edge): the run timer. It shrinks, and late on it shows a "0:15" pill (16). A bite costs "-10s" (13). A "+6s" potion refills it (09).
- **NOISE** bar: green, then orange, then red, ending in a teeth icon. Moving too fast fills it, and full means it bites.
- **IN BAG**: loot you've grabbed from this chest but not yet kept.
- **BANKED**: loot that's safe.
- **CHAIN x1.2 / x1.5**: a multiplier for banking in a row without waking it ("Hush Chain", 15).
- **Nameplate**: mimic name plus traits, like "Heavy sleeper", "Light sleeper", "Snorer", "Dreamer · fake twitches", and a tier ("Wooden / Gilded gold x2 / Royal gold x3"). The traits hint at how touchy each one is. That's nice, but nothing explains what they actually change.
- **Zzz / eye**: the mimic's state. Zzz means asleep, a half-open slit means stirring (06), the full cat eye means awake (12).

## 4. Why does it bite, and what does it cost?
You make too much noise (lever moved too fast, maybe grabbing too fast) and the NOISE bar hits red. "Slower..." warns you first (12). The bite (13) shows "CHOMP! Too loud!", takes the whole bag (-179, IN BAG goes to 0) and burns 10 s off the candle. Banked gold is safe. That reads clearly and fairly.

## 5. How do you keep loot?
Slide the lever down to shut the lid. That banks the bag ("Hushed! BANKED +1,407", 15; "BANKED +975", 16). Banking while it's still asleep counts as "Hushed" and feeds the chain multiplier.

## 6. Does it feel like a complete, polished £1.99 game?
Mostly yes. The chest art is warm and consistent. Mimic names like Dozy Doris and Wheezy Walt have charm, and the gilded and royal chests are a nice escalation. The wake-up eye is a great "uh-oh" beat. The results screen has a per-chest breakdown and a flavour line ("You lifted the Lullaby Music Box..."). There's a codex with funny relic names, badges plus Game Center, a daily mode, ranks, and proper settings (sound on silent, reduce motion). That's a real game, not a toy.

What still feels unfinished or cheap:
- 14-bank: the whole bottom half goes flat black for a moment. The chest body art, lock and interior vanish and only the HUD floats on darkness. It looks like a rendering bug.
- 07: after the "bank it" lesson, IN BAG = 0 and BANKED = 0 and the loot has just vanished. My first bank seems to have paid nothing. It feels broken.
- 27-cover: about 2/3 of the screen is empty gradient under "Open me... gently". It feels unfinished or like a placeholder.
- 24-settings: a big empty void between Credits and Done. Fine, but sparse.
- 15-relic: the frame is named "relic", but I can't see which relic I got or any "new relic!" moment. The flying loot is just a blur over the BANKED box.

## 7. Confusing, unreadable, misaligned, cut off or ugly (by frame)
- **01 / 02 / 25 home**: "Slide the lid up... slowly." sits under a static chest on the home screen, where the real action is the Play button. I tried to slide the chest. Either make the chest slidable to start, or change the line.
- **03**: the in-world "Slide this up... slowly" is printed across the mimic's eyelid in yellow on pink and is hard to read. It says the same thing as the panel below ("Slide the lever up... slowly"), so I got the instruction twice.
- **06** (lesson 4, "freeze"): the panel already says "Slide the lever down to keep it." and the progress dots show step 5. I never saw a "stop / freeze when it stirs" instruction. The lesson seems skipped or mislabelled.
- **07 / 08**: the lever knob is hidden under the pink lip. In 08 it sits below the tongue, half off the track.
- **05 / 08 / 20 / 25 / 27**: a black Dynamic Island pill sits on top of the chest lid art in 05 and 08. Not the game's fault, but the art could avoid that zone.
- **09 / 10 / 14 / 15 / 16**: the "CHAIN" label and x1.2 badge are jammed against the right screen edge, and the badge is partly clipped.
- **15 / 16**: flying loot covers the "BANKED" label ("BANK..." / "BAN..ED"), and a "59+" tag sits on top of it.
- **16**: the "0:15" pill overlaps the candle tube and looks stuck on.
- **13**: the teeth come down over the brass hinge plates and into the bottom half. That's dramatic, but the teeth overlap the HUD frame and it looks messy.
- **20 results**: the middle stat card ("2 Hushed banked while it slept") is taller and sits higher than its neighbours, so the row is misaligned. A cut-off panel edge peeks out above the Home/Again buttons, so it looks like there's hidden content I can't tell how to reach. The last chest tile shows "0" with a glowing gold chest, which is confusing: was that the music box?
- **23 codex**: the third row is staggered because "Sir Rattlesworth's Skull" wraps to three lines and makes its card taller. The bottom row is faded and cut off by the Done button. It says "22 of 24 found" while home 02 says 9/24 (I'm guessing different save states).
- **40 badges**: locked icons are nearly black on dark teal and very hard to make out. The Relic Hunter icon looks like a tombstone, and the Heavy Bag icon looks like a music box rather than a bag.
- **10 vs 09**: BANKED drops from 1,118 to 197 between frames. If that's the same run, it's alarming.

## 8. Would the app icon make me tap it?
Probably yes. A chest with teeth and one orange eye peeking out says "mimic" instantly and is cute-creepy. Complaints: it's flatter and simpler vector art than the rich in-game chest, so it feels like a different artist. The lid straps don't line up with the body straps. The two coin lumps on the rim look like yellow blobs, not coins. The dark edges will disappear on a dark home screen.

## 9. Scores (/10)
- Understood how to play: **8**
- Want to play: **7**
- Looks finished / premium: **6.5** (the black-out bank frame, the lost first bank, clipped CHAIN and empty screens drag it down)
- Would pay £1.99: **7** (yes once those glitches are fixed; today a bit "nearly there")

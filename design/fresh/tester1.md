# Fresh tester 1 - first-time player read (stills only)

Game appears to be called **Hush - "Don't wake the mimic"** (from the home screen, which I saw near the end).

## 1. What is the goal?
You're a little thief robbing treasure chests that are actually sleeping monsters (mimics). You open the lid, grab
the loot without waking the monster, then close it to keep the loot. You do as many chests as you can before your
candle burns out. You get the most gold you can without getting chomped.

## 2. How do you control it?
My best guess: hold the phone folded like a little laptop. The top screen is the monster's lid (eye, teeth, name
plate) and the bottom screen is the inside of the chest. "Slide the lid up... slowly" opens it. Then I drag each
coin or gem with my finger down into the leather strap/bag area at the bottom. When it says "Freeze!" I stop moving.
"Slide it down to keep it" closes the lid and banks the loot.
I am genuinely NOT sure if "slide the lid up" means swipe my finger up on the top screen (the pointing-hand icon
and "Slide up here" box say finger) or physically open the fold wider (the phone folding like a laptop says hinge).
I'd probably try swiping first.

## 3. The bar near the bottom
A "how awake is the monster" meter. The little face on the left is a sleeping face. It fills green, then orange,
then red. When it's full red the monster wakes and goes CHOMP. Moving fast fills it ("Slower...").

## 4. The candle/torch
It's your time limit for the run. It burns down as you play, and when it's gone the run is over. (The game calls it a
"torch", but it's clearly drawn as a candle.)

## 5. What made the monster bite?
The wake meter filled to red. I think that happened because I moved too fast or kept moving after it said
"Freeze!". In 13-bite the meter is full red, the eye is wide open, the teeth slam down, and my bag goes from
164 to 0.

## 6. Keeping loot / banking
The loot in your bag (left counter, the sack icon) isn't safe yet. Closing the lid ("slide it down to keep it")
banks it, and it moves into the gold pile (right counter, the coin stack). A bite loses whatever's in the bag
but not what's banked. The results screen confirms it: "1,019 gold went down the hatch".

## 7. The score
Gold banked for the run (e.g. 1,683), plus a Best score. There are also stats (chests banked, "Hushed", bites),
a rank ladder (Pickpocket -> Sneak -> Lockpick...), achievements, and a relic collection (Codex, 24 relics).

## 8. Which half do you lift?
The TOP half. It's the lid with the eye, the teeth and the name plate. The bottom half is the chest's insides
where the loot sits. But see the confusion in #2: I'm not certain I physically lift it rather than swipe.

## 9. Confusing / ugly / unreadable / I'd get wrong
- **34-lesson-1-fold**: the whole screen is upside down. As a new player I'd think the game is broken or I'm holding
  it wrong. Nothing tells me to flip or fold the phone, even though the frame seems to be about folding.
- **34 / 03-lesson-1-pose**: "Slide up here" plus a finger icon vs a phone that folds. I don't know whether to swipe
  or open the hinge. "Slide the lid up... slowly" doesn't settle it.
- **04-lesson-2-open**: "Drag the coin into your bag". The "coins" look like copper cinnamon buns / bread rolls,
  not coins. And where is "my bag"? There's no obvious bag on screen, just a leather strap and a small sack icon in
  the corner. I'd likely drag it to the sack icon at the bottom-left.
- **05-lesson-3-grab**: faded ghost text "Dozy Doris / Wooden chest x1" is printed right on top of the loot.
  It looks like a rendering bug and makes the loot harder to see. There are now 4 items, but the tip still says
  "the coin".
- **06-lesson-4-freeze**: the "Dozy Doris / Wooden chest x1" title again sits on top of the loot. "Freeze!" is clear,
  but I'd still be holding my finger on an item. Do I let go or hold still?
- **07-lesson-5-bank**: "Slide it down to keep it". What is "it", the lid or the loot? The tip box also covers the
  wake meter, so I can't see the meter while I'm being taught about it.
- **08-lesson-6-card**: says "torch" but the picture is a candle. A tall gold gauge with a knob also appears on the
  top-right of the lid, and nothing explains it.
- **09-run-ready vs 10-run-reveal**: my banked gold shows 379, then 179, at the start of runs. Where did that gold
  come from on my "first run", and why did it go down? The numbers feel random.
- **14-bank**: shows "+179" but the total reads 151. Probably mid-count-up, but on a still it looks wrong.
- **15-relic vs 16-torch-low**: gold 1,852, then 1,819. It goes down even though banked gold shouldn't drop?
- **15-relic**: "Hushed!" (green), "Relic found!" and "+948" all pop up at once and compete with each other.
  I don't know what "Hushed" means. Is it a bonus for not waking it at all?
- **19-results-fresh**: a huge empty gap in the middle of the screen. "0 Hushed" isn't explained. "Rank up!
  Sneak" has a tiny progress sliver. It looks a bit unfinished compared to the in-game art.
- **01-home-fresh**: the chest drawing is flat, simple vector art and looks like a placeholder next to the rich
  in-game chest. "Codex 0/..." is cut off. The rank progress bar under "Pickpocket Rank 1" is basically invisible.
  It says "Drag the lid up" here but "Slide the lid up" in the tutorial (small inconsistency).
- **22-codex-mid**: the subtitle "9 of 24 found. Iron-bound chests and up hide them." runs off both edges of
  the screen. The locked silhouettes repeat the same crown, goblet and teacup shapes I've already found, so it looks
  like duplicates. The relic icons are much plainer than the in-game art.
- **26-daily**: nothing on screen says this is the Daily Dungeon. It looks exactly like a normal run. A floating
  "+10" overlaps the bag counter.
- In general, the top ~17% and bottom ~17% of every in-game frame is empty black, so the game feels letterboxed
  and smaller than the screen.

## 10. Scores (/10)
- I understood what to do: **6/10**. Steal loot, don't wake it, bank it is clear and charming. *How* to
  physically open the lid (swipe vs fold) and the upside-down first frame aren't.
- I want to play it: **7/10**. The tension of the eye opening, the meter and the CHOMP is a good hook, the
  monster names are cute, and collecting relics appeals to me.
- It looks finished/premium: **6/10**. The in-game chest art is lovely and polished. The menus (home, results,
  codex) look plainer and have clipping, truncation and ghost-text bugs.
- I'd pay £1.99: **5/10**. Maybe, if the fold gimmick feels great in my hands. From stills alone it's a
  nice-looking mini-game, but I'm not sure it has enough variety to justify paying up front.

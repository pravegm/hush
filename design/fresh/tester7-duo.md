# Tester 7 — first-time Duo player (stills only)

Frames viewed in order: 34, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 07b, 39, 08, 31, 11, 33, 13, 13b, 14, 37, 36, 15, 16, 38, 30, 22, 26, 25, 27. I mentally rotated the upside-down fold frames (34, 39, 31, 33, 30).

## 1. Goal
Rob sleeping mimic treasure chests. Open each one quietly, drag the coins and gems into your bag, then close it to "bank" them before the chest wakes up and bites. Your candle is the run timer, and you're trying to bank as much gold as you can before it burns out. You also collect relics for a Codex (9/24), plus there are badges, ranks (Lockpick, then "Cat Burglar") and a Daily Dungeon.

## 2. What I physically do
- **Phone:** stand it up like a little laptop and turn it round (the fold frames render upside down until you do). Then slowly lift the top half, which is the chest lid / mimic face, to open it, and ease it shut to bank. The mimic's mouth opens and closes with the hinge.
- **Fingers:** drag loot from the open chest down into the bag. The non-fold frames (03–08, 11, 13–16, 26) seem to swap the hinge for an on-screen lever: slide it up slowly to open, slide it down to bank.

## 3. Which half to lift, and how I knew
The top half once the phone is turned round, meaning the half with the mimic's face. In 34 that half has a gold outline and a "Lift this half" tag with an arrow, and the pose screen (25) shows a glowing top half with an up arrow: "Lift the glowing half. Keep the other half flat, nearest you." It was clear, but only after I'd mentally flipped the frame.

## 4. Terms
- **NOISE:** a meter that goes green, then orange, then red, ending in teeth. Opening too fast or too far fills it. Red means it bites.
- **IN BAG:** loot you've grabbed but haven't secured yet ("not safe yet"). You lose it if you get bitten.
- **BANKED:** gold that's safe for good. It's your score.
- **Candle:** the run timer. "Candle out = run over." Potions add time (+6s) and bites burn it (−10s). A 0:15 countdown shows up when it's low (16).
- **HUSH CHAIN:** a streak multiplier for banking while the mimic is still asleep. "Sleepy banks pay more." It went x1.1, x1.2, x1.4, x1.5 over a run.

## 5. Bites
Too much noise (a fast or wide open) wakes it up. The eye opens through "It's stirring. Freeze!" and "Slower…", and then you get CHOMP! "Too loud!" (13). A bite costs your whole bag (−179) plus 10 seconds of candle. I assume it also resets the Hush Chain.

## 6. Golden chest
These are fancier chests, gilded or royal with gold trim and jewels, and they carry a "GOLD x2" or "GOLD x3" coin on the nameplate, so they pay double or triple. Iron-bound chests were x1.5 (37). The results strip (38) highlights one gold chest (+6,354), which reads as the jackpot chest.

## 7. "Hushed"
You banked a chest while the mimic was still asleep, so it never woke. The 07b banner says "Hushed!", and the results screen counts "14 Hushed while it slept". That's what keeps the Hush Chain going.

## 8. Confusing, unreadable, cut off or ugly
- **25 vs 34/30:** the pose screen says "Turn it sideways", but the game actually wants the phone turned upside down (180°). I'd turn it 90° on my first try.
- **Two control schemes:** 08 says "Slide the lever down to bank" and 39 says "Ease the lid shut to bank". I couldn't tell whether the on-screen lever still works when the phone is folded.
- **07b:** the banner says "BANKED +119", but the counter shows 115, and "Hush Chain x1.1" is shown next to a badge reading "GOLD x1.2". Which multiplier is real?
- **07b, 14, 37, 36, 15, 16, 31:** the HUSH CHAIN badge label is clipped at the right edge ("HUSH CHAI…").
- **14, 36:** the top tip banner ("Hush Chain! Sleepy banks pay more", "Slide the lever down to bank") runs edge to edge and jams into the pause button.
- **07:** a flying ruby covers the "IN BAG" label ("IN E").
- **05:** it says "Drag the coin" (singular), but four items are on screen. Which one?
- **34, 31, 33, 30 (fold):** the padlock shows a "!" instead of a keyhole, and I don't know what that warning means.
- **13 / 13b:** these look nearly identical. I couldn't see a separate "creak" warning beat.
- **38:** it says 14 banked chests, but the strip shows 11 tiles plus "+4 more" (15). The second row of values is greyed and hard to read, and a chevron overlaps "+6,354".
- **08 / 39:** "Not yet, take me home" is an odd label for the end of a tutorial.
- **27:** thin lines across the frame above and below the chest, with a stray "z" clipped at the top line. It looks like a cropping glitch.
- **Fold frames (31, 33, 39):** the in-chest tip text is small, and on the stood-up bottom half it's at a sharp angle.

## 9. Scores
- Understood: **8/10**. The tutorial really carries it.
- Want to play: **8/10**. Lifting a real lid against a mimic is a great hook.
- Looks finished / premium: **7/10**. The art is lovely and consistent, but the HUD has several clipped and overlapping bits.
- Would pay £1.99: **7/10**. Yes on a Duo, if the hinge feels as good as it looks.

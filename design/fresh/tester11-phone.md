# Tester 11: first-time player, normal iPhone (stills only)

Frames viewed in this order: 01, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 07b, 08, 09, 10, 12, 13, 13b, 14, 14b, 15, 16, 19, 20, 02, 22, 23, 24, 40, 43, 44, app-icon-1024.

## 1. Goal
Each chest is a sleeping mimic. I open its lid a little, drag coins and gems into my bag, then bank them before it wakes up. One run goes through a series of chests (Wooden, then Gilded, then Royal) until the candle burns out. I want the most gold banked, plus relics for the Codex and badges. I got this from the tagline and the first lesson without trouble.

## 2. Controls
- A vertical lever on the right of the mimic. Pushing it up opens the lid. Pulling it all the way down banks what is in the bag.
- Drag loot from the open chest into the bag in the middle.
- Stop moving ("Freeze!") when it starts to stir.
- A pause button.
What confused me a bit: the home screen says "Slide its lid up... slowly" (01, 02, 44) but the game says "Slide the lever up" (03, 09). That's two different words for one control. Also, using the same lever to open and to bank wasn't obvious until card 08 spelled it out.

## 3. Meters and labels
- NOISE bar, which turns into STIRRING and then WAKING!, with a teeth icon at the end. It fills green, then orange, then red. Clear.
- Candle on the left: time left. A 0:10 countdown shows up when it's low (16). Clear.
- IN BAG ("not safe yet"), BANKED, and CHAIN x1.1 / x1.5. I could work out IN BAG and BANKED. CHAIN was only explained by a banner that flashed past: "Sleepy banks pay more".
- Name plate (Dozy Doris "Heavy sleeper", Wary Wendell "Light sleeper"), plus GOLD x2 / x3 badges on the fancier chests. Fun, and I could tell it's a difficulty hint.
- The eye opening across 06, 12 and 13 says "danger" very well.

## 4. Why it bites, and what it costs
Too much noise (moving too fast, I assume) fills the meter to the teeth. Then it's CHOMP!, "Too loud!", and you lose everything in the bag (-179) plus 10 seconds of candle (13). Banked gold stays safe. The bite screen makes all of that clear, and that's good.

## 5. How to keep loot
Pull the lever all the way down to bank. Banking while it's still asleep builds the Hush Chain multiplier. Only banked gold counts at the end.

## 6. Does it feel like a complete, polished paid game?
Mostly yes. The art is consistent and charming. The chest gets fancier as you go (wood, then gold trim, then royal jewels). The mimics have names. There's a 24-relic Codex with funny names ("Key to Nothing", "Ring of Mild Invisibility"), 12 badges, a Daily Dungeon, ranks (Pickpocket, Lockpick, Cat Burglar), Game Center, and a strong results screen with a per-chest history strip. That's well beyond a cheap toy.
What still feels unfinished or cheap:
- "Hushed!" pops up on every bank (07b, 14), yet the results say "0 Hushed while it slept" after a run where I banked six times (19). The word "Hushed" means two different things, so the stat reads like a bug.
- Relics: the "Relic found!" card (15) never says what a relic does or whether it counts as gold. It seems to be collection only, but the game never tells me.
- Golden mimic: it shows up in the results legend and on a badge ("Midas Touch"), but it's never introduced in play in what I saw.
- The tutorial shows the bag at 24 (07), then "BANKED +119" (07b). The jump isn't explained. Maybe I grabbed more loot off-screen, but as a still frame it looks wrong.
- The tutorial end card (08) offers "Not yet, take me home", which is odd wording right after I've finished the lesson.
- The results screen lists both causes, "The mimic bit you 5 times. Candle out." (19). Which one ended the run?

## 7. Confusing, unreadable, misaligned, cut off or ugly
- 13b: the "Freeze!" banner and the "CREAK!" text are stacked right on top of each other and crowd each other.
- 14: the "Hush Chain! Sleepy banks pay more" banner almost touches "Hushed!" below it. The CHAIN x1.1 pill runs into the riveted plate on the right, and a small stub of the pill sticks out past it.
- 15: the relic card's right-hand handle overlaps a coin or object peeking out behind it. The GOLD x2 medallion sits over the left end of the name plate. It looks deliberate, but it's busy.
- 16: the top banner covers a purple gem (only half of it shows), and the gems along the top rim are clipped. The 0:10 chip sits on top of the bottom of the candle.
- 13: on the bite, the lower teeth row runs straight across the top of the loot window and over the brass hinges. It's dramatic but a bit messy.
- 07, 12, 16: "not safe yet" is very small and sits tight against the bottom of the IN BAG box.
- 20: a half-visible card ("NEW...") is cut off under the fade above the chevron. It reads as clipped, not as "scroll for more".
- 40: the "Heavy Bag" badge icon looks like a little person or statue, not a bag.
- 44 (large text): Badges is cut off at the bottom, and Settings and Game Center are pushed off-screen. The chevron helps, but it feels cramped. 43 (large text) is fine.
- App icon: strong and readable, with the eye peeking through the teeth. A coin covers a strap rivet, which is minor.

## 8. Settings
Sound, Vibration (Off/Normal/Strong), Play sound on silent, Reduce motion and Replay First Chest are all clear.
"Lid sensitivity: Forgiving / Normal / Careful" is the one I had to think about. "How much noise the lid makes" suggests Careful means a noisier lid (harder), but "Careful" could also read as "the lid is careful", meaning quieter. "Forgiving / Normal / Strict" or "Easy / Normal / Hard" would be clearer. The note that the scored Daily always uses Normal is good. I didn't open About or Credits.

## 9. Scores (/10)
- Understood: 8
- Want to play: 8
- Looks finished / premium: 8
- Would pay £1.99: 8

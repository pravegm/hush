# Tester 10: first-time player, normal (non-folding) iPhone, paid £1.99

I only looked at the still frames, in the order I was given. I did not read any code, docs or captions.

## 1. Goal?
Each chest is a sleeping mimic. I open its lid, drag the coins and gems into my bag, then bank them before it wakes up. A run ends when my candle burns out, and my score is the gold I banked. The chests get fancier as I go (Wooden, then Gilded at x2 gold, then Royal at x3), and some of them hold relics I can collect in the Codex. The subtitle "Don't wake the mimic" told me most of this before I even pressed Play.

## 2. Controls?
- There is a vertical lever on the right of the mimic's face. Sliding it up opens the lid, and I'm meant to go slowly.
- I drag loot from inside the chest into the bag at the bottom middle.
- Sliding the lever all the way down banks what's in the bag.
- When it says "Freeze!" I have to stop moving.
- A pause button sits at the top right.

The tutorial (03-08) taught each step one at a time and was easy to follow. One thing threw me: the home screen says "Slide its lid up" (01), but in the game I'm actually sliding a lever (03).

## 3. Meters and labels
- **NOISE bar.** It goes green, then orange, then red, and ends in a set of teeth. The bar's label changes to STIRRING and then WAKING! (06, 12), which is a nice touch.
- **Candle** on the left. This is the run timer. A 0:09 countdown shows up when it gets low (16).
- **IN BAG.** Loot I'm holding. It says "not safe yet" underneath.
- **BANKED.** Loot that's safe.
- **CHAIN GOLD x1.1 / x1.5.** I think this goes up when I bank without being bitten.
- **GOLD x2 / x3** medallion on the mimic's nameplate.
- **Mimic name and temperament**, like "Dozy Doris: Heavy sleeper" or "Wary Wendell: Light sleeper". I take this as a difficulty hint.
- **Rank bar** on the home screen (Pickpocket, Lockpick, Cat Burglar) and my best score.

## 4. Why does it bite, and what does it cost?
It bites when I'm too loud: opening the lid too fast or not freezing in time. The "CHOMP! Too loud!" screen (13) showed the whole bag lost (-179) and -10s off the candle. What I had already banked stayed safe. This was clear and felt fair.

## 5. How do I keep loot?
Slide the lever down to bank it. Banked gold is safe for the rest of the run. The tutorial card (08) says it plainly: candle out = run over, too loud = it bites your bag, lever down = bank.

## 6. Does it feel like a complete, polished paid game?
Mostly yes. The cartoon art is charming and consistent all the way through: the eyelid, the teeth, the brass plates, the wood grain. The chests clearly get more upmarket over a run. There's a lot of extra stuff too: a Codex of 24 relics with funny names, 12 badges, a Daily Dungeon, ranks, Game Center, and a results screen with a strip showing each chest. That feels worth £1.99.

What still feels unfinished or cheap:
- The game screen is very busy. There's chrome above, below and on both sides, and the actual chest interior where I play is fairly small.
- The hint banners cover the top of the loot area (09, 10, 16).
- The CHAIN chip is tiny and squeezed in beside the noise bar's teeth (10, 14, 14b, 15).
- Some badge icons don't match their names. Heavy Bag shows a music box and Light Fingers shows a goblet (40).
- In the tutorial, my bag showed 24, but the banked popup said +119 (07 to 07b). I couldn't tell where the extra came from.

## 7. Confusing, unreadable, misaligned, cut off or ugly (frame names)
- **44-home-axl.** With large text the home buttons break: "Today' / s try r...", "Codex 9...", "Badges..." are cut off, and "Game Center" touches the edge of its button. This is the worst one.
- **07-lesson-5-bank.** The flying ruby covers the "IN BAG" label. "not safe yet" is very small and low-contrast.
- **14-bank.** Coins flying in cover the "BANKED" label so it reads "ANKED". This is mid-animation, but it looks messy.
- **13-bite.** The teeth slam down over the hinges and the pause button. It works, but it's cluttered.
- **15-relic.** The gold straps run behind the Zzz and the mimic panel. The relic card overlaps the right-hand strap.
- **16-torch-low.** The 0:09 pill sits on top of the candle base, and the burnt-down candle is hard to read.
- **20-results-mid.** The chest strip's second row is cut off by the fade, and the last three chests show no values. I'd have to know to scroll down.
- **41-results-axl / 45-results-xxxl.** "it slept" fades out at the bottom of its tile, and the chest strip is pushed off the screen. Oddly, XXXL comes out smaller than AXL.
- **23 / 42 / 46 Codex.** "Sir Rattlesworth's Skull" is in a smaller font than every other relic name.
- **08-lesson-6-card.** The gold side bars stick out past the edges of the card.
- **04, 09, 02.** A black Dynamic Island blob sits over the top wood. That's fine on a real phone, but there's a lot of dead wood above the nameplate.
- **24 / 43 / 47 settings.** Fine. Lots of empty space, but nothing is broken.

## 8. Would the icon make me tap it?
Yes. A chest with a yellow eye peeking through its teeth and coins spilling out reads instantly as "mimic" and looks cute. It's clean and bold and would stand out on a dark home screen. One tiny nit: a coin at the bottom right half-covers a rivet.

## 9. Scores (out of 10)
- Understood: 9
- Want to play: 8
- Looks finished / premium: 7.5
- Would pay £1.99: 8

# Tester 9 (Duo owner, first look, stills only)

Frames viewed in order: 34, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 07b, 39, 08, 31, 11, 33, 13c, 13, 14, 14b, 37, 36, 36b, 15, 16, 38, 30, 22, 26, 25, 27.

## 1. Goal
The game is called "Hush" ("Don't wake the mimic"). Every treasure chest is a sleeping mimic. You open it, take as much loot as you can into your bag, then shut it and bank the loot before it wakes up. You keep going from chest to chest until the candle burns out. Score = gold banked (18,926 in frame 38). The chests get fancier as you go (Wooden, Iron-bound, Gilded, Royal) and you collect relics for a Codex.

## 2. What I physically do
- With the Duo standing like a laptop, I turn it round so the hinge is horizontal (frames 34/39/31/33 are upside down in raw capture). The far half that stands up is the mimic's lid and face. The near half lying flat is the inside of the chest, holding the loot, the bag and the bank.
- I lift or open the standing half slowly to open the lid. I drag coins and gems with a finger from the chest into the bag. Then I ease the lid shut to bank the loot.
- On a flat phone the same thing is done with a vertical lever slider on the mimic half (frames 03-08). Slide it up slowly to open and down to bank.

## 3. Which half do I lift, and how did I know?
The mimic half, with the face, teeth and name plate. Frame 34 has a "Lift this half" tag with an arrow on that half, and the half also has a glowing gold outline. The pose screen (25) says "Lift the glowing half. Keep the other half flat, nearest you. That's the floor." Its little drawing shows the lid with an up arrow. That was clear.

## 4. HUD
- **NOISE**: a meter that fills green, then orange, then red, with a set of teeth at the end. Its label changes to STIRRING and then WAKING!. Moving the lid fast and a full, jingling bag ("Full bags jingle. Bank soon.") seem to fill it. The mimic's eye opening by stages matches the meter.
- **IN BAG**: loot you are holding. It says "not safe yet", so you lose it if bitten.
- **BANKED**: safe gold that counts toward your score.
- **Candle**: the run timer on the left edge. "Candle out = run over". A bite takes -10s off it, and it shows a 0:09 countdown when low ("Candle's low. Bank it!").
- **CHAIN**: banking without waking it builds a "Hush Chain" multiplier (x1.1, x1.2 ... x1.5), shown as "CHAIN GOLD x1.3" beside the noise meter.

## 5. What makes it bite, and what it costs
Too much noise: the meter reaches the teeth, or you keep moving after "Freeze!" / "CREAK!". It costs the whole bag (CHOMP! Too loud! -179, bag goes to 0) plus 10 seconds of candle. I assume it also breaks the chain.

## 6. Golden chest
A rare golden mimic ("A golden mimic! Extra gold!"), shown with a gold-tinted chest, a green gem on the hinge and a "GOLD x3" medal. Each loot piece shows x3. On results, "glow = golden mimic" marks it in the chest strip.

## 7. "Hushed"
It means you banked while the mimic stayed asleep: a clean, quiet steal. Results counts "14 Hushed while it slept", and a zzz over a chest in the strip means the same thing. I liked it.

## 8. Confusing, unreadable, cut off or ugly
- **25 pose**: "Turn it sideways" is wrong for what I actually do. The fold frames are turned right round (180 degrees), not sideways. I'd try landscape first. Something like "Turn it round" or "Spin it so the hinge is across" would fix it.
- **39 lesson card (fold)**: the card is pushed against the screen edge. "Not yet, take me home" is clipped at the edge, and the card covers the pause button.
- **30 home (fold)**: the bottom row (Settings / Game Center) is clipped by the screen edge.
- **08 vs 39**: the card's third tip says "Slide the lever down to bank" in one and "Ease the lid shut to bank" in the other. Fine if it matches my pose, but the lever lessons (03-07) don't mention lifting at all. If I start flat and stand it up later, will I know to lift?
- **Multiple "GOLD xN" badges**: the mimic's plate medal (x1.5 / x2 / x3) and the CHAIN "GOLD x1.3" use the same wording. I can't tell what multiplies what, or whether they stack. Royal (16) is also "GOLD x3", the same as the special golden mimic (36), which makes the golden one feel less special.
- **CHAIN badge (14, 31, 37)**: tiny text crammed between the teeth icon and the frame edge. In fold mode (31) it is near unreadable.
- **07 / 14**: flying loot covers the "IN BAG" / "BANKED" labels mid-animation ("ANKED"). Minor.
- **37**: "Hushed! BANKED +429 / Hush Chain" fades and overlaps "Next chest..." in the same spot. It looks muddy.
- **31 / 33 (fold)**: the "Drag the loot" and "Freeze!" banners sit right on the hinge edge of the flat half. On a real Duo the fold gap may cut into them.
- **13 bite**: the teeth slam across the hinge and over the pause button. The effect works, but it's busy.
- **26 daily**: the "DAILY DUNGEON · OCT 27" ribbon is off-centre and overlaps the chest-name plate.
- **38 results**: the chest strip's second row and the "14 more" tile are faded and cut off. You need to know you can scroll.
- On a first try I'd probably over-lift and get bitten, because I wouldn't realise "slowly" means VERY slowly. That's part of the fun.

## 9. Scores (/10)
- Understood: 8
- Want to play: 8. The physical lid-lift on a Duo is a genuinely clever idea.
- Looks finished/premium: 7. The art is charming and consistent, but the clipped cards, the crowded CHAIN badge and the overlapping text pull it down.
- Would pay £1.99: 7

# Tester 6 — foldable iPhone, first-time player (screenshots only)

Viewed 21 captures in order: duo-ui-* then duo-h* then duo-0*. I flipped the inner-screen shots 180° in my head, as the game tells you to. The cover-screen shots (duo-ui-cover, duo-04) are already the right way up.

## 1. Goal?
Hush, "Don't wake the mimic". The phone is a treasure chest, and the chest is a sleeping mimic. You open the lid slowly and drag the coins, gems and potions into your bag before your candle burns out, without waking it. Then you bank the gold. Over a run you go through several chests (my results screen showed 8 banked and 4,449 gold), rank up (Lockpick, Cat Burglar, Shadow) and collect relics for a Codex.

## 2. What do I physically do?
I stand the phone up like a little laptop. The lower half sits flat and is the inside of the chest, with the loot, candle, noise bar and bag. The upper half is the lid, with the mimic's sleepy face and a nameplate ("Wheezy Walt, Snorer, Wooden"). I raise the lid slowly from roughly 105° to 160° to see more of the inside, and then hold it still. Every prompt says "slowly" or "gently", so a slow, steady open of a couple of seconds is the pace.

## 3. Which half do I lift, and how did I know?
The upper half, the lid. The first lesson (duo-ui-lesson-1-150) puts a gold glowing frame around that half with "Lift this half" and an arrow pointing to its top edge. The pause card (duo-h3) shows "Lift me" with an arrow in the same place. The art helps too: the lid half shows the mimic's face, and the flat half shows the inside of the chest.

## 4. Fingers?
One finger on the flat lower half: "Drag the loot into your bag". The bag is the pouch in the middle of the bottom edge, between IN BAG and BANKED. There's also a gold pause button in the corner of the loot area. I don't touch the lid half.

## 5. Meters and labels
- **NOISE bar** (lower half, under the lid edge): it fills from the NOISE label towards a red mimic-teeth icon. My guess is that once it reaches the teeth, it bites.
- **Candle** (tall gauge at the side): your time. It burns down during the run, and potions tagged "+6s" add time.
- **IN BAG**: loot you have grabbed but not banked yet. **BANKED**: loot that's safe. I'm not sure what action banks it. Probably closing the lid gently, which would explain "Hushed = banked while it slept" on the results screen.
- **Nameplate** ("Dozy Doris, Heavy sleeper"; "Wheezy Walt, Snorer"): each chest's personality, I guess meaning how easily it wakes.
- **Home**: Rank and progress bar, Best gold, Daily Dungeon, Codex x/24, Badges x/12, settings, and an unlabelled flask button.

## 6. Too fast? Closing the phone?
- **Too fast** (duo-h2-after-slam, duo-h2-slam-bite): the NOISE bar jumps by 5 to 7 segments, the lid's teeth peek out and an eye half-opens. The loot disappears from the inside. If you're loud enough it bites, and the run ends (results count "Bites").
- **Closing**: below about 55°, the game dims and pauses with a card: "Lift the lid a little to carry on. The mimic waits. Your candle does too." (duo-h3). With the phone fully shut, the cover screen says "Open to carry on. Open me gently, then hold still." (duo-04). Opening it again picks up where you left off (duo-h4, duo-05). That's clear and reassuring.

## 7. Problems (file by file)
- **duo-ui-lesson-1-150**: mixed messages. The lid says "Lift this half" with an arrow, but the tip box on the other half says "Close it a little, nearly upright…". Do I lift it or close it? The tip box also covers the top of the loot area.
- **duo-h2-after-slam / duo-h2-slam-bite**: the noise from a slam shows up GREEN, and green reads as "good". There's no red, no shake and no "Too loud!" text. The mimic reacts very little (teeth just poke out), and the loot quietly vanishes. I couldn't tell if I'd been bitten, warned, or lost the loot. This is the most important moment in the game and it's the weakest feedback.
- **duo-h3-closing-card-55**: the pause card covers most of the NOISE bar, so only "SION" shows. The "Open the lid… slowly" toast still sits under the card, and the lid says "Lift me", so there are three prompts at once. "Home" is plain small text that doesn't look like a button.
- **duo-ui-codex**: the header says "22 of 24 found", but Home says "Codex 9/24", which looks like a bug. The bottom row of relics is cut off behind the Done button (half-hidden labels), and the top half is mostly empty.
- **duo-ui-settings**: the "Replay First Chest" row is half-hidden behind Done. The top half has only the word "Settings". "Keeps the loot on the half that stays sharp when you fold" is jargon. What does "stays sharp" mean?
- **duo-ui-pose-book**: it's identical to the home screen. No pose or "hold it like this" instruction is visible, so if one was meant to appear, it didn't.
- **duo-ui-home-120 / -150**: the flask button has no label, so I don't know what it is. Otherwise it looks good.
- **duo-06-half-135-unturned**: this is the same screen the default way up. If someone turns off "Turn the chest round" while holding the phone the way the game asked, everything is upside down in their hands. Fine as long as nobody touches that setting.
- **duo-ui-results**: 9 chests are shown with the last one at "0", but it says 8 banked and 0 bites. Why did the run end? I assume the candle ran out, but it doesn't say. Small issue.
- **Orientation**: once flipped, all the in-game text reads correctly, including the nameplate, the "+6s" tags and NOISE/IN BAG/BANKED. Nothing would look upside down in my hands with the default setting on.

## 8. Scores /10
- Understood: **7**. Lift the lid slowly and drag the loot is very clear. Banking, and what a slam actually cost me, are not.
- Want to play: **8**. It's a charming idea, and the fold really is the chest lid.
- Looks finished/premium: **8**. The chest art, the mimics and the results screen are lovely. Clipped rows, the Codex count mismatch and weak slam feedback pull it down.
- Would pay £1.99: **7**. I'd pay for this, especially once the slam/bite moment is dramatic.

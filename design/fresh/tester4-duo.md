# Tester 4 (foldable, first-time player, images only)

## 1. Goal
Steal as much gold as possible from sleeping mimic chests ("Hush - Don't wake the mimic") before the candle burns out. Open each chest slowly, drag loot into the bag, shut it to bank, then move on to the next chest. Score = gold banked; new best, rank (Lockpick > Cat Burglar), and relics to collect (9 of 24 in the codex).

## 2. What I physically do
- Turn the phone round and stand it up like a laptop (fold frames 34/31/33/39/30 are upside down until I rotate them). The bottom half is the inside of the chest plus the HUD. The top half is the lid with the mimic's eye.
- Open the lid slowly. On the foldable I *think* that means lifting the top half, because in the fold frames (31, 33) the lid half has no slider, and 39 says "Shut the lid to bank it". In the flat frames (03-07) it's an on-screen brass slider on the right that I move with my thumb.
- Fingers: drag coins, gems and potions from the chest into the bag icon. Then close the lid (or slide the lever down) to bank.

## 3. Which half do I lift, and how did I know
The top half, the one with the eyelid, the name plate and the teeth along its edge. I knew because the art is a chest: wooden lid with a latch up top, lock plate on the bottom half, brass hinges at the fold, teeth on the lid's lip. But the game never actually says "lift the top half". The fold tutorial (34) says "Slide the lever up... slowly" / "Slide this up... slowly" next to an empty outlined slot, and home (30) says "Slide the lid up". So I'd be 70% sure it means the hinge and 30% expecting a finger swipe.

## 4. HUD meanings
- NOISE: how loud I'm being (opening too fast, grabbing too hard?). It goes green, then orange, then red, and the teeth icon at the end is the bite point.
- IN BAG: loot I'm holding but haven't secured yet, so it's at risk.
- BANKED: loot made safe by shutting the lid. That's my score.
- Candle: the run timer. "Candle out = run over". A 0:15 countdown shows when it's low (16), and potions give "+6s" (11).
- CHAIN: a streak multiplier for banking chests without waking them (x1.2 > x1.4 > "Hush Chain x1.5").

## 5. Bite
Noise reaching red / the eye fully opening (33 shows a red tint, eye open, "Slower..." as the warning). In 13 the cost is "CHOMP! Too loud!": the whole bag is lost (-179, IN BAG back to 0) and the candle loses 10s. I assume the chain resets too, but nothing shows it.

## 6. Golden chest
Not clear. Name plates show tiers: Wooden < Iron-bound (gold x1.5) < Gilded (gold x2) < Royal (gold x3). The results strip (38) highlights one gold chest worth +6,354, so I'd guess a golden chest is a rare high-multiplier jackpot chest. Frame 36 ("golden-mimic") shows an ordinary wooden "Puffing Percy" with one sparkly ruby, so I couldn't tell what was golden about it.

## 7. "Hushed"
A chest I robbed and shut without it waking up. The "Hushed!" banner shows on a clean bank (15), Zzz icons sit over each chest in the results, and the results card says "14 Hushed".

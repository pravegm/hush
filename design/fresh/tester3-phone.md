# Tester 3 — first-time player, normal (non-folding) iPhone, paid £1.99

I only looked at the still frames, in the order I was given them. I didn't read any code or docs.

## 1. What's the goal?
Each chest is a sleeping mimic. You open it quietly, steal the loot inside and get the loot home safe before your candle ("torch") burns out. Chests get fancier as you go (wooden, then iron-strapped, then gold and jewelled with a crown) and each has a silly name: Dozy Doris, Wary Wendell, Wheezy Walt, Mumbling Milo. Your score is the gold you bank in one run, and you go up ranks (Pickpocket, Cat Burglar, then Shadow).

## 2. Controls on my phone
- Drag the lid (top half) **up slowly** to open it (03 "Slide up here").
- Drag each coin or gem **down into the bag** at bottom-left (04/05).
- When the eye opens and you see "Freeze!", **stop touching or moving** (06/12).
- Slide the lid **down** to close the chest and keep the loot (07).
It all made sense to me, but only because the tutorial spelled out each step.

## 3. The meters
- **Candle** (left edge): your time for the run. It burns down, and in 16 it's almost gone.
- **Noise / wake bar** (row of squares under the chest, sleepy face on the left): goes green, then orange, then red as the mimic wakes up.
- **Bag** (bottom-left number): loot you've grabbed but haven't kept yet.
- **Bank** (bottom-right, coin stack): loot that's safe.
- The eye in the lid acts as a second wake meter: closed, then a slit, then wide open.

## 4. Why would it bite?
You make too much noise, probably by moving the lid or loot too fast or not freezing when it stirs. The bar goes red, the eye opens wide, the teeth drop and you get "CHOMP!" (13). The bag drops to 0, so you lose whatever you hadn't banked.

## 5. How do you keep loot?
Close the lid (slide it down) while the mimic is still asleep. The bag empties into the bank: "+179" floats up and the bank counts up (14). It's push-your-luck: grab more and risk the bite, or bank now.

## 6. Complete, polished paid game?
Mostly yes. The in-game chest art is lovely: velvet mouth, tongue, teeth, a slit-pupil eye and brass plaques. The names and writing are charming ("Lovely work, little thief.", "The Ring of Mild Invisibility"). There's a lot around the core game: Daily Dungeon, a codex with 24 relics, ranks, achievements and settings with haptics and reduce-motion.

What feels cheap or unfinished:
- In every game screen the game sits in a band in the middle of the phone with **huge black bars top and bottom** (about a third of the screen is empty). It looks like a layout made for a different screen size and shrunk to fit.
- The **pause button is half off the right edge** in every game frame.
- The art on the home and cover screens is a **plain flat chest**, much simpler than the in-game chest. It looks like placeholder art.
- The **codex icons are basic shapes**: the dice is a white square, the compass a brown circle, and the ring looks like a bottle.
- There's a **"stand it up like a little laptop" screen (25)** that my phone can't do, and it's cut off anyway.
- The **trophy button** seems to lead nowhere: 40-badges is just the home screen.

## 8. App icon
Yes, I'd probably tap it. A chest with a glowing eye peeking out under a row of teeth reads as "mimic" straight away and it's fun. The warm brown and gold stand out against the dark teal. Small niggles: the gold coins on the rim are blobby, and the lid's teeth and eye get small at icon size. It's still one of the better parts of the package.

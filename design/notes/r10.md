# R10: the visual regressions after the orientation rounds (integration: App + Scene + CI scenarios)

Source: `design/VISUAL-REGRESSION-R9.md` (findings R1 to R6), checked against `artifacts/ci17s`, `ci9s`, `duo8` and
`duo10`. The R6–R9 orientation and never-stuck rules are untouched: no change to k, the insets turn, the scene turn,
the scale mode, touches, pauses, the watchdog or the masks. Nothing is committed.

## R1 (P1): the pause coin and its brass seat now have one source of truth
- **Cause (confirmed):** the coin is `AppCanvas.pauseCenter()`, clamped into the turned safe rect. The seat was
  `ChestLayout.pauseCenter(viewScale:)`, which only clamps to the canvas edge. At k = 1 on the Duo the window's inset
  edge lands on the canvas's right side, so only the coin moved in (duo-06).
- **Fix:** `AppCanvas.pauseSeatCanvasCenter()` is the coin's centre turned back into canvas points
  (`CanvasFit.canvasPoint(view:)`, the inverse of `viewPoint(canvas:)`). RootView's `screenLayer` hands every drawn
  frame to `GameController.placePauseSeat(_:)` (`onAppear` and `onChange(of: info)`), which sets
  `ChestScene.pauseSeatCanvasCenter`. A newly made scene picks it up in `makeScene`. The scene's seat
  (`SceneFloorHUD.placeSeat`) sits there, sized by `viewScale`. When the value is nil (scene tests, before the first
  layout), it falls back on the old clamp. Without side insets the old clamp gives the same point (tested).
- The hint plate is now measured against the real seat: `HUDLayout.hintPlateRect(seat: info.pauseSeatCanvasRect())`.
  `hintPlateRect(viewScale:)` is kept and delegates to it.
- Test `R10Tests.pauseSeatSitsUnderTheCoinForEveryQuarterTurnAndInset` covers both Duo windows × k 0…3 × 4 Duo-like
  inset sets. It checks that the scene-drawn seat centre is within 1 pt of the SwiftUI coin in window points, that
  the seat radius is the coin's radius plus the ring, and that the plate keeps `pauseGap`.

## R3 (P2): Settings
- `PageScaffold` now uses `FadingScroll` (the Results scroll) instead of a fixed mask that always faded the last 10 %.
  The fade and the "⌄" show only while more content is below. Content that fits is never faded, which fixes frame
  24's Credits looking disabled. The bottom padding went from 72 to `scrollFoot` = 12, and the last row always
  scrolls clear of Done. Codex, Badges and Daily get the same scroll.
- On a Duo (`SettingsView.splitsAcrossHalves`), the lid half holds the title and the sound-and-feel card in its own
  fading scroll (`PageScaffold.lidExtra`). That card is Sound, Vibration, Lid sensitivity and Play sound on silent.
  The floor half holds Turn the chest round, Reduce motion, the Replay / About / Credits card and Done. A phone keeps
  one column with the same rows.
- `SettingChoice` pills keep `pillPadding` (14 pt) either side of the name, with `minimumScaleFactor` 0.7. At the
  accessibility sizes they stack one per line at full width (`stacksPills`), so "Forgiving" at AXL no longer fills
  its pill.

## R4 (P2): the Results hero mimic
- The portrait scene draws the chest 330/400 of the frame's height wide (`ResultsView.chestWidthPerPortraitPoint`).
  `minPortrait` went from 60 to 110, so the chest is at least 90.75 pt wide on a phone. On the Duo the lid-half
  portrait is `duoPortrait(lidHeight:)` = 0.4 × the half height, capped at 190 (≈ 178 pt, so a ≈ 147 pt chest; it was
  ≈ 120 pt).
- To pay for the bigger chest, the outcome ("New best!", "First haul!", "N short of your best", "Best N") now shares
  the "gold banked" row (`ViewThatFits`, falling back to stacked). That saves about 36 pt, more than the 32 pt the
  bigger floor adds, so the strip's two rows plus "+N more" stay above the fade at the default size. At AXL and XXXL
  the 110 pt floor wins: the strip may need a scroll there, and the chevron says so.

## R5 (P3): the empty seat fades with the coin
- Rule `AppRules.pauseCoinHidden(menuOpen:closingPaused:finalLessonCard:biting:)`, shared by PlayLayer and the
  controller. `GameController.advance` sets `ChestScene.pauseSeatHidden` every frame.
- `SceneFloorHUD.seatTarget` returns 0 whenever the coin is hidden, including during the bite (the coin hides then
  too). The node is hidden below 1 % alpha.

## R6 (check): the "ghost ruby" in frame 26 is not a bug
- In `logs/26-daily`, the last event before the freeze is `itemPicked id=1 kind=gem.ruby`. The expert pilot drags
  while the lid opens, straight down to the bag row (ThiefPilot: `ty = bagY`, x ± 40). The ruby is held mid-drag
  over the front rim at about canvas (513, 130), within `labelFade` (16 pt) of the BANKED label. That is why it is
  drawn at about 0.4 alpha (R5 `labelClearAlpha`, floor 0.3).
- Dropped items are not animated back (they draw at their chest position), and lost items fly to the mouth. Only
  held items and the pouch hop fade, so no dropped or returning item can fade over the HUD. Pinned by
  `SceneTests.heldGemOverTheRimIsOnlyLabelFaded`. The 26 caption says so.

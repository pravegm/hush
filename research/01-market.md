# Research 01: market, concept choice, constraints (27 Oct 2026)

## Brief
Fourth iPhone Duo-first paid game from the studio (after Squish, Brim, Swoop). £1.99 one-off, no ads, no IAP, no data.
Must use the hinge; must be unique; "simple, fun, addictive, satisfying". Built unsupervised.

## Duo hinge landscape (FoldHunt, iThinkDiff, Notebookcheck, T3; Oct 2026)
Taken mechanics, so avoided:
- Angle matching by feel: **Duometry**, **Obtuse**.
- Bend to charge, open to launch: **FoldFling**.
- Fold pumps a marble's swing: **Valley Run** ("Fold to Fly").
- Bowling slope follows the hinge: **Uphill**.
- Fold switches modes: **NEOFOLD** (brick breaker, pinball, duel).
- Paper folds with the phone: **Kami**, **Duo Cut**, **PureOrigami**.
- Close to chop: **Exactly Half**.
- Close to block, open to cast: **Tensei Mahō Shōjo** (spellbook).
- Pop-up book: **Foldtales**.
- Fold to squeal: **FoldAhhh**.
- Piano tiles in laptop pose: **PianoFold**.
- **Hinge as accordion bellows: Fold Accordion** ($2.99, 24 songs, Learn mode), **Foldissimo**, **Accorduon**,
  **Bellowsby**, **Foldeon**, **DuoBox**.
- Our own games: Squish (close to crush), Brim (tabletop, lift to pour), Swoop (book pose wings, fold to dive).

**Not found anywhere:** a game where hinge *speed* (gentleness, stealth) is the skill, and any chest, lid or mimic
game.

## Concept choice
- The concept agent scored 14 concepts plus an accordion rhythm game (see the decision log).
- The accordion idea was the studio founder's own first pick. It was dropped after this research: hinge-as-bellows is
  taken six times over.
- **Chosen: the mimic chest (67/80, rank 1).**
  - The Duo is a treasure chest, and the raised half is the lid.
  - Open it slowly to see the loot. Lift the loot out gently, then ease the lid shut to bank it. Don't wake the
    mimic.
  - Why it won:
    - Gentleness *is* the skill, so it is the kindest game for the hinge (about two slow strokes per chest).
    - It is tolerant of the ±5° angle error, because speed matters, not an exact angle.
    - It reads in 5 seconds.
    - Push-your-luck is a proven loop.
    - Its clip moment is the mimic waking.
- **Risk:** it shares tabletop pose with Brim and Squish. The answer is a different verb (restraint and stealth, not
  angle or crush), a dungeon palette and a mascot.

## Hard constraint from the founder (27 Oct, mid-build)
"When the Duo screen is folded, the left half gets blurred for a second or so."
- Prior evidence from Brim: in iOS portrait the blurring half is the **bottom** of a tabletop.
- Brim build 8 drew the tabletop turned round (the player turns the Duo over), and the founder confirmed it works.
- Our rule: **the lid is the blurring half; the chest floor (loot, meters, warnings) is the sharp half.** Every
  time-critical cue is also given in sound and haptics, and is repeated on the floor.

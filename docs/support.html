Support

Need a hand, or found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub. A real person reads every one, and we aim to reply within two working days. You'll need a free GitHub account. Issues are public, so please don't include personal details. You can also browse existing issues to see if someone has asked already.

Playing

How do I play on iPhone Duo?

Stand the Duo on a table like a little laptop: one half lying flat (the chest's floor, with the loot), the other half raised (the lid). The first time, Hush shows you which half to lift. Open the lid slowly and the light spills into the chest; the wider it opens, the deeper you can see. Drag the lit loot down into your bag, then ease the lid back down and hold it there for a moment to bank it. Your first chest is a short lesson, First Chest, which shows each step.

How do I play on other iPhones?

Hold your iPhone upright. The top half of the screen is the lid, with a brass lid slider on it: slide it up slowly to open, down to shut. Everything else is the same: drag the loot into the bag, slide the lid down and hold it to bank. Every chest, relic, rank and achievement works this way.

Why did the mimic wake up?

Noise. The noise meter on the chest floor fills when you move the lid fast, drag loot fast, or drop loot outside the bag, and it drains when you keep still. Listen for the snoring: when it stops and a heartbeat starts, it's stirring, so slow down. When the meter turns red and FREEZE appears, stop moving and it settles. The longer you stay in one chest, the lighter it sleeps, and Light Sleepers wake more easily than Heavy Sleepers (check the name tag).

It bit me. What did I lose?

Everything in your bag that you hadn't banked yet, and 15 seconds of torch. Gold you've already banked is safe. The mimic hops away and the next chest arrives.

How do I bank my loot?

Ease the lid back down until it's nearly shut, with at least one thing in your bag, and hold it there for a moment. A ring fills on the bag, then the haul is counted and the next chest comes. Close it gently: closing too quickly is noisy too.

When does a run end?

When your torch burns out. Torch oil found in some chests adds time; a bite takes 15 seconds away. Anything still in your bag when the torch goes out is lost, so bank before it does.

Is this OK for my hinge?

Yes. Hush is about moving the hinge slowly: about two slow strokes per chest, between nearly shut and comfortably open, in the middle of the range the Duo's hinge is made for. It never asks you to lay the phone flat, close it or move it fast. Moving fast is how you lose. Never put anything real on the screen.

Half the screen went blurry for a moment

That's iOS: when a fold starts, iPhone Duo briefly softens one half of its inner screen. It isn't Hush, and no app can switch it off. So Hush draws the chest turned round, and asks you to lift the half that softens as the lid. The loot, the noise meter and the warnings stay on the half that stays sharp. If on your Duo the floor is the half that goes blurry, open Settings > Turn the chest round and switch it, then lift the other half.

It paused and says "Lift the lid a little"

You closed the Duo further than play needs. Lift the lid back up a little and the run carries on. If you close it fully, the outer screen shows the sleeping mimic until you open it again.

It says "That's wide enough"

You've opened the lid wider than any loot needs. There's no penalty: ease it back a little. There's never a reason to open the Duo flat.

It asks me to stand it up like a little laptop

Hush is played in tabletop pose, not held sideways like a book. Stand the Duo on a table, one half flat and the other raised, and the game carries on.

How does the Daily Dungeon work?

The same chests for everyone that day. Your first try of the day is the one that counts for your result and the Daily Dungeon leaderboard; you can practise as much as you like afterwards.

Relics, the Codex and thief ranks

Relics sit deepest in the Iron-bound chests and better, so you'll need to open the lid wide to reach them. There are 24, and each one stays in your Relic Codex once you've banked it. Every coin you bank also counts towards your thief rank: twelve ranks, from Pickpocket to Mimic Whisperer.

Sound, feel and settings

No sound, or too much?

Hush plays sound even with the silent switch on. To make it follow the silent switch, turn on Respect silent switch in Settings. Sound and Haptics each have their own switch. Every warning is shown on screen too, so you can play with the sound off.

Reduce motion

Settings has Reduce motion, which calms the screen shake and the flash when the mimic bites.

Replay the lesson

Settings > Replay First Chest.

Game Center

Optional. If you're signed in, your Best Haul and your Daily Dungeon result go to the leaderboards, and achievements are reported as you earn them. Hush works fully without it.

Purchases and your progress

Will I have to pay again later?

No. One price, no in-app purchases and no ads. Updates are free.

Family Sharing?

Yes, Hush is available through Family Sharing.

Did I lose my progress?

Your progress lives only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone's own backups). Deleting the app deletes it, and we can't restore it, because we never had it.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reporting a bug

Open an issue with:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About);
  • whether you were playing with the hinge or the lid slider;
  • the mode you were playing (Run, Daily Dungeon or First Chest);
  • what happened, and what you expected;
  • a screen recording or a short video, if you can. It helps a lot.

Support

Need a hand, or found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub. A real person reads every one, and we aim to reply within two working days. You'll need a free GitHub account. Issues are public, so please don't include personal details. You can also browse existing issues to see if someone has asked already.

Playing

How do I play on iPhone Duo?

Stand the Duo on a table like a little laptop: one half lying flat (the chest's floor, with the loot), the other half raised (the lid). The first time, Hush shows you which half to lift. Open the lid slowly and the light spills into the chest; the wider it opens, the deeper you can see. Drag the lit loot down into your bag, then ease the lid back down and hold it there for a moment to bank it. Your first chest is a short lesson, First Chest, which shows each step.

How do I play on other iPhones?

Hold your iPhone upright. The top half of the screen is the lid, with a brass lever on it: slide it up slowly to open, down to shut. Everything else is the same: drag the loot into the bag, slide the lever down and hold it there a moment to bank. Every chest, relic, rank and achievement works this way.

Why did the mimic wake up?

Noise. The noise meter on the chest floor fills when you move the lid fast, drag loot fast, or drop loot outside the bag, and it drains when you keep still. Listen for the snoring: when it stops and a heartbeat starts, it's stirring, so slow down. When the meter turns red and Freeze! appears, stop moving and it settles. The longer you stay in one chest, the lighter it sleeps; loot in your bag keeps a little noise in the air, so a full bag never lets it settle completely; and Light Sleepers and golden mimics wake more easily than Heavy Sleepers (check the name tag).

It bit me. What did I lose?

Everything in your bag that you hadn't banked yet, and 10 seconds of candle. It also breaks your Hush Chain. Gold you've already banked is safe. The mimic hops away and the next chest arrives.

How do I bank my loot?

Ease the lid back down until it's nearly shut, with at least one thing in your bag, and hold it there for a moment. The haul flies into BANKED and the next chest comes. Close it gently: closing too quickly is noisy too.

What's the Hush Chain? And the golden mimics?

A chest you bank without the mimic ever stirring is Hushed. Hushed banks in a row build a Hush Chain that pays more gold each time: ×1.1, ×1.2, up to ×1.5. One stir or a bite breaks it. From the third chest on, now and then a golden mimic turns up: its gold is worth double, but it sleeps lighter.

When does a run end?

When your candle burns out. A flask of oil found in some chests adds 6 seconds when you put it in your bag; a bite takes 10 seconds away. The candle stops while the game is paused. Anything still in your bag when the candle goes out is lost, so bank before it does.

Is this OK for my hinge?

Yes. Hush is about moving the hinge slowly: about two slow strokes per chest, between nearly shut and comfortably open, in the middle of the range the Duo's hinge is made for. It never asks you to lay the phone flat, close it or move it fast. Moving fast is how you lose. Never put anything real on the screen.

Half the screen went blurry for a moment

That's iOS: when a fold starts, iPhone Duo briefly softens one half of its inner screen. It isn't Hush, and no app can switch it off. So Hush draws the chest turned round, and asks you to lift the half that softens as the lid. The loot, the noise meter and the warnings stay on the half that stays sharp. If on your Duo the floor is the half that goes blurry, open Settings > Turn the chest round and switch it, then lift the other half.

It paused and says "Lift the lid a little to carry on"

You folded the lid down past upright (below about 70 degrees). That's a pause: the candle stops and the mimic's noise stays where it was. Lift the lid back up past upright and hold it still for a moment (the card says "Hold still…"), and the run carries on. If you close the Duo, or iOS moves Hush to the outer screen, that's a pause too: the outer screen shows the sleeping mimic until you open it again.

It says "That's wide enough"

You've opened the lid wider than any loot needs. There's no penalty: ease it back a little. There's never a reason to open the Duo flat.

It shows a card that says to lift the glowing half

Hush is played in tabletop pose, not held sideways like a book. Stand the Duo on a table, one half flat, and lift the half the card shows glowing. If the chest doesn't appear within a couple of seconds, tap I've stood it up.

How does the Daily Dungeon work?

The same chests for everyone that day. Your first try of the day is the one that counts for your result and the Daily Dungeon leaderboard; you can practise as much as you like afterwards.

Relics, the Codex and thief ranks

Relics sit deepest in the Iron-bound chests and better (about one chest in four), so you'll need to open the lid wide to reach them; "Something glints deeper…" means one is there. There are 24, and each one stays in your Relic Codex once you've banked it. Every coin you bank also counts towards your thief rank: twelve ranks, from Pickpocket to Mimic Whisperer.

Sound, feel and settings

No sound, or too much?

Hush plays sound even with the silent switch on. To make it follow the silent switch, turn off Play sound on silent in Settings. Sound and Haptics each have their own switch. Every warning is shown on screen too, so you can play with the sound off.

Reduce motion

Settings has Reduce motion (Hush also follows the iOS Reduce Motion setting). It removes the screen shake and the red flash when the mimic bites.

Replay the lesson

Settings > Replay First Chest.

Game Center

Optional. If you're signed in, your Best Haul and your Daily Dungeon result go to the leaderboards, and achievements are reported as you earn them. Hush works fully without it.

Purchases and your progress

Will I have to pay again later?

No. One price, no in-app purchases and no ads. Updates are free.

Family Sharing?

Yes, Hush is available through Family Sharing.

Did I lose my progress?

Your progress lives only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone's own backups). Deleting the app deletes it, and we can't restore it, because we never had it.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reporting a bug

Open an issue with:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About);
  • whether you were playing with the hinge (iPhone Duo) or the lever;
  • the mode you were playing (Run, Daily Dungeon or First Chest);
  • what happened, and what you expected;
  • a screen recording or a short video, if you can. It helps a lot.

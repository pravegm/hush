Support

Need a hand, or found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub. A real person reads every one, and we aim to reply within two working days. You'll need a free GitHub account. Issues are public, so please don't include personal details. You can also browse existing issues to see if someone has asked already.

Playing

How do I play on iPhone Duo?

The phone is the chest. When the phone is shut, the chest is closed; open the phone even a little and the chest opens with the phone. There's no special way to hold it. Three rules:

  1. The chest opens with the phone. Move it slowly.
  2. Drag the loot into your bag.
  3. Close the phone to bank it before your candle runs out.

To start with the phone shut, the outer screen shows these rules ("Steal the mimic's gold while it sleeps") and a Play button: press Play, then open the phone slowly. Or open the phone first and press Play on the inner screen: the chest starts open. After you bank, the outer screen says Banked +N gold! and the next chest waits, closed: open the phone slowly for it. There's no lesson to sit through; if you'd like a guided chest, Settings > Replay First Chest.

How do I play on other iPhones?

Hold your iPhone upright. The top half of the screen has a brass lever on it:

  1. In a chest, slide the lever up slowly to open it.
  2. Drag the loot into your bag.
  3. Slide the lever down to bank it before your candle runs out.

Then slide the lever up again for the next chest. Every chest, relic, rank and achievement works this way.

Why did the mimic wake up?

Noise. The noise meter on the chest floor fills when you open the phone (or slide the lever) fast, drag loot fast (the game says Slower…), or drop loot outside the bag, and it drains when you keep still. Listen for the snoring: when it stops and a heartbeat starts, it's stirring, so slow down. When the meter turns red and Freeze! appears, stop moving and it settles. The longer you stay in one chest, the lighter it sleeps; loot in your bag keeps a little noise in the air, so a full bag never lets it settle completely; and Light Sleepers and golden mimics wake more easily than Heavy Sleepers (check the name tag).

It bit me. What did I lose?

Everything in your bag that you hadn't banked yet, and 10 seconds of candle. It also breaks your Hush Chain. Gold you've already banked is safe. Close the phone (or slide the lever down) for the next chest.

How do I bank my loot?

With at least one thing in your bag, close the phone (on other iPhones, slide the lever back down). The haul flies into BANKED and the next chest waits, closed; open the phone (or slide the lever up) for it. With loot in your bag, closing is the bank itself, so it never wakes the mimic. (A fast close over an empty bag is still heard.)

What's the Hush Chain? And the golden mimics?

A chest you bank without the mimic ever stirring is Hushed. Hushed banks in a row build a Hush Chain that pays more gold each time: ×1.1, ×1.2, up to ×1.5. One stir or a bite breaks it. From the third chest on, now and then a golden mimic turns up: its gold is worth double, but it sleeps lighter.

When does a run end?

When your candle burns out. A flask of oil found in some chests adds 6 seconds when you put it in your bag; a bite takes 10 seconds away. The candle stops while the game is paused and while the phone is shut. Anything still in your bag when the candle goes out is lost, so bank before it does.

Is this OK for my hinge?

Yes. Hush is about moving the hinge slowly: the phone's ordinary open and close, done gently. It never asks you to lay the phone flat or move it fast. Moving fast is how you lose. Never put anything real on the screen.

Half the screen went blurry for a moment

That's iOS: when a fold starts, iPhone Duo briefly softens one half of its inner screen. It isn't Hush, and no app can switch it off. So Hush keeps the loot, the noise meter and the warnings on the half that stays sharp. If on your Duo the loot ends up on the half that goes blurry, switch Settings > Turn the chest round.

It seems stuck

On iPhone Duo, close the phone and open it again slowly: the chest follows the phone. If a card says "Open the phone slowly to carry on", that's all it needs; after 3 seconds it also offers Carry on (play on straight away) and Home. If the phone is open but the outer screen's card is still showing, wait 3 seconds: Already open? offers Carry on and Home. With the phone shut mid-run, the outer screen offers End run (your banked gold is kept). On other iPhones, slide the lever down and up again, or tap the pause coin and choose Home. If it still seems stuck, send us the log (below).

How do I pause?

Use the round brass pause coin. On iPhone Duo, during a chest, press and hold it for a moment (a ring fills), so the hand on the phone can't pause by accident; a quick tap just shows "Hold to pause". Closing the phone also stops the candle. On other iPhones, a tap pauses.

How should I hold my Duo?

However is comfortable: on a table or in your hands. There's no pose to hold. Hush plays whichever way iOS turns the screen and draws the chest the right way up. There's never a reason to open the Duo flat: no loot needs it.

How does the Daily Dungeon work?

The same chests for everyone that day. Your first try of the day is the one that counts for your result and the Daily Dungeon leaderboard; you can practise as much as you like afterwards.

Relics, the Codex and thief ranks

Relics sit deepest in the Iron-bound chests and better (about one chest in four), so you'll need to open the chest wide to reach them; "Something glints deeper…" means one is there. There are 24, and each one stays in your Relic Codex once you've banked it. Every coin you bank also counts towards your thief rank: twelve ranks, from Pickpocket to Mimic Whisperer.

Sound, feel and settings

No sound, or too much?

Hush plays sound even with the silent switch on. To make it follow the silent switch, turn off Play sound on silent in Settings. Sound has its own switch, and Vibration can be Off, Normal or Strong. Every warning is shown on screen too, so you can play with the sound off.

It wakes too easily, or not enough

Settings > Lid sensitivity: Forgiving (for shaky hands: easier to stay quiet), Normal or Strict (for thieves who never creak). The scored Daily Dungeon try always plays Normal, so it stays fair for everyone.

Reduce motion

Settings has Reduce motion (Hush also follows the iOS Reduce Motion setting). It removes the screen shake and the red flash when the mimic bites.

Is there a tutorial?

No forced one: the three rules are on screen, and your first run is the real thing. For a guided chest (open, grab, freeze, bank), use Settings > Replay First Chest any time.

Game Center

Optional. If you're signed in, your Best Haul and your Daily Dungeon result go to the leaderboards, and achievements are reported as you earn them. Hush works fully without it.

Purchases and your progress

Family Sharing?

Did I lose my progress?

Your progress lives only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone's own backups). Deleting the app deletes it, and we can't restore it, because we never had it.

Reporting a bug

Open an issue with:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About);
  • whether you were playing with the hinge (iPhone Duo) or the lever;
  • the mode you were playing (Run, Daily Dungeon or First Chest);
  • what happened, and what you expected;
  • a screen recording or a short video, if you can. It helps a lot.

If it ever seems stuck: open Settings > About, press and hold the Version line for 2 seconds, tap Copy log, and paste it into a new issue. The log is a short list of what the game saw (pauses, resumes, the screen turning, the hinge angle). It stays on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere unless you copy and paste it yourself.

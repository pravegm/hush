How it plays

On iPhone Duo

Stand the Duo like a little laptop: one half flat, the other raised. The raised half is the lid. Open it slowly and the candlelight spills in, deeper the wider you go. Drag the loot into your bag gently, then ease the lid down to bank it. About two slow strokes per chest. It plays the right way up whichever way the screen turns. Fold it down past upright, or close it, and the run pauses, candle and all; it carries on once the lid is open past upright and still, or straight away with Carry on.

On every other iPhone

A brass lever on the lid does the hinge's job: slide it up slowly to open, down to bank. Same chests, same mimic, same leaderboards. On the Duo you feel the lid creak under your fingers, but nobody is left out.

Don't wake it

Every chest starts asleep. The noise meter on the chest floor fills when you move too fast and drains when you keep still. The creak, in sound and in your fingers, follows your exact speed.

  • AsleepIt snores. The velvet tongue rises and falls. Loot away.
  • StirringThe snoring stops, a slow heartbeat starts and the tongue twitches. Slow down.
  • WakingThe meter turns red, Freeze! pulses and the heartbeat quickens. Hold still and it settles.
  • CHOMPToo late. The teeth snap shut across the fold. Everything in your bag is lost, and your candle loses 10 seconds.

Loot in your bag keeps a little noise in the air, so the more you carry, the less the mimic settles. Each mimic wears a name tag and has a habit: Snorers, Light Sleepers, Heavy Sleepers, and Dreamers who twitch in their sleep.

Push your luck

Coins sit near the front, gems further back and relics deep inside: when something glints deeper in, open wider for it, or bank what you have. Your candle burns down while you loot (a flask of oil buys six more seconds), and the chests get richer as you go, from Wooden to Iron-bound, Gilded and Royal. When the candle goes out the run ends, and anything still in your bag is lost.

Sleepy banks pay more

Bank a chest without the mimic ever stirring and it's Hushed. Hushed banks in a row build a Hush Chain: gold ×1.1, ×1.2, up to ×1.5. One stir breaks it. Now and then a golden mimic turns up: it doubles a chest's gold, but it sleeps lighter.

Ways to play

  • Run: chest after chest until your candle burns out. Runs last about two minutes.
  • Daily Dungeon: the same chests for everyone, every day, and one try that counts. Then practise as much as you like.
  • First Chest: a short lesson with a very heavy sleeper. Replay it from Settings any time.

Collect

  • Twenty-four named relics for your Relic Codex, from the Moth-Eaten Crown to the Idol of the Sleepy God, each found once and kept.
  • Twelve thief ranks, from Pickpocket to Mimic Whisperer, earned by every coin you bank.
  • Game Center leaderboards for your Best Haul and the Daily Dungeon, and twelve achievements (optional).

Made for the Duo's fold

iPhone Duo softens one half of its screen for a moment when a fold starts. So Hush draws the chest turned round: the half that softens is the lid, and the loot, the noise meter and every warning stay on the half that stays sharp. The game shows you which half to lift, and Settings → "Turn the chest round" changes it if your Duo differs.

It's kind to the hinge, too. Hush never asks you to lay the phone flat, close it or move it fast. Moving fast is how you lose.

Pay once. That's it.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking and no data collected. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Every sound, from the creak to the CHOMP, is made live in the app, and every warning is shown on screen too, so you can play with the sound off.

## 1. The decisions

| # | Topic | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **App name** | `Hush: Don't Wake the Mimic` (26/30), plain ASCII apostrophe | The game's title (`research/02-names.md`); bare "Hush" is crowded by sleep apps and Flavourworks' *HUSH* |
| 2 | **Fallback names** | `Hush: The Mimic Chest` → `Tiptoe: Don't Wake the Mimic` → `Hush - Don't Wake the Mimic` | "Tiptoe" is the research's runner-up; the home-screen name stays "Hush" unless the trademark check says otherwise |
| 3 | **Trademark** | The founder searches UK IPO and USPTO for "HUSH" in classes 9, 28 and 41 before creating the record (runbook §1a). A live mark for games or game software → switch to "Tiptoe" before anything is public | `research/02-names.md`: "not checked"; Flavourworks' *HUSH* is a game, so a games-class mark is a real possibility. Not legal advice; a solicitor only if a mark turns up |
| 4 | **Subtitle** | `Open slowly. Steal the gold.` (28/30), both locales | The verb and the goal in five words; indexes open/slowly/steal/gold; works for hinge and lever. Alternative: `The game where you're gentle` (28) |
| 5 | **Promotional text** | Duo pose first, then the lever, then no ads/IAP (170/170) | If the Duo screenshot slot is missing, this, frame 1 and the description carry the Duo pitch; editable any time |
| 6 | **Description** | Opens "Your iPhone Duo is a treasure chest. The chest is a sleeping mimic.", then the loop, the store line, no ads, then sections, including the Hush Chain and golden mimics; 3,935/4,000 (en-US: 2 spellings) | The design's 5-second pitch first. Every line traced to the code in `metadata.md` §10 (R3) |
| 7 | **Keywords** | `treasure,chest,loot,thief,stealth,sneak,dungeon,gentle,monster,hinge,foldable,lid,tiptoe,relic,heist` (100/100 bytes) | No repeats of name/subtitle words, no competitor or Apple marks, no "creak" (Amanita's *Creaks*), singulars only |
| 8 | **Categories** | Games → **Casual** + **Adventure**; secondary **Entertainment** | Casual is where paid one-input games chart. **Adventure, not Puzzle** (the brief's suggestion): nothing in Hush is a puzzle, the Puzzle chart is the most crowded on the store, and dungeon/treasure/thief is Adventure's audience. Arcade has left Apple's live list (Brim, 26 Oct). One-word change to Puzzle if the founder prefers |
| 9 | **Age rating** | **9+**: Cartoon or Fantasy Violence **Infrequent**, Horror/Fear Themes **Infrequent**, Contests **Infrequent** (leaderboards, as Brim); everything else None/No; not Made for Kids | The bite is comic, with no injury, but it is a monster's teeth slamming shut with a red flash after a heartbeat build-up: mild cartoon peril and a mild fear theme. 4+ means *none*. Under-rating risks rejection and angry parents; 9+ costs nothing for a paid game bought by adults (`metadata.md` §8) |
| 10 | **Price** | Base **UK £1.99** (Apple's £1.99 price point), Apple equalises the rest ($1.99). No launch discount | The brief's price; same as Brim and Swoop |
| 11 | **Territories** | All **except China mainland and Vietnam** | Paid games need an NPPA number in China and a game licence in Vietnam; Hush has neither (Brim's COMPLIANCE.md #15). Swoop's runbook excludes only China; Brim's later check found Vietnam too |
| 12 | **Mac and Vision Pro** | Untick both | No hinge, the lever untested with a mouse or gaze (Brim's COMPLIANCE.md #16) |
| 13 | **Release type** | **Manual release**, pressed by the founder on **Tue 1 Dec 2026, about 08:00 UK** | The brief's choice. Manual lets the founder hold the release if a late Duo problem shows up, and fits a launch week the founder controls. (Brim used Scheduled; the tool supports both) |
| 14 | **Launch date** | **Tue 1 Dec 2026**; submit **Wed 25 Nov** (last safe day Fri 27 Nov) | Brim Tue 17 Nov, Swoop Tue 24 Nov: one launch a week gives each its own news and featuring slot. 35 days out: 3 weeks' featuring notice (by Tue 10 Nov) and room for two or three real-Duo tuning rounds. Clear of US Thanksgiving (Thu 26 Nov) for launch, and early December is gift-and-new-phone season. Note: Swoop's kit called 1 Dec "Brim's fallback"; Brim was submitted on 26 Oct and is scheduled for 17 Nov, so that fallback is moot, but if Brim slips to 1 Dec, Hush moves to 15 Dec |
| 15 | **Fallback date** | **Tue 15 Dec** if 1.0 isn't submitted by Fri 27 Nov; never 8 Dec (Swoop's fallback) | Keeps the one-launch-per-week rule across the studio; still before the App Store's Christmas review slowdown |
| 16 | **Featuring** | App Launch nomination, publish date 1 Dec, **submitted by Tue 10 Nov** (sooner is better); "Optimized for iPhone Duo" in Helpful Details | Apple asks for 3 weeks' notice and asks Duo apps to use that phrase |
| 17 | **Duo screenshots** | Upload D1–D6 (2007 × 2853) **if** App Store Connect shows the iPhone Duo slot; otherwise ship 1.0 with the 6.9" and 6.3" sets and add the Duo set in 1.0.1 (before April 2027, when it becomes required) | Apple lists the slot; forum 845505 reported none in the UI or API in October; screenshots lock at approval |
| 18 | **Frame 1** | A CI **tabletop composite**: D1's real capture warped onto a stylised Duo standing like a laptop, lid raised ~130°, no hands; flat D1 as the fallback | The pitch is physical (the raised half is the lid); a flat frame can't show it. Swoop's `--book` composite is the proven method to port |
| 19 | **Daily Dungeon day boundary** | Recommend **00:00 UTC** for everyone | Game Center's recurring board resets at one UTC moment; Brim and Swoop made the same change |
| 20 | **Leaderboards** | `hush.best` Classic "Best Haul" and `hush.daily` Recurring daily from **1 Nov 2026 00:00 UTC** "Daily Dungeon"; Integer, High to Low, best score, suffix " gold" | The score is gold banked (`GAME-DESIGN.md` §4); starting 1 Nov lets TestFlight testers see the board |
| 21 | **Achievements** | 12 (`hush.ach.*`) **exactly as the code has them** (`Achievements.all` in `Hush/Core/Ranks.swift`; R2 renamed `royal` to Midas Touch and `hushhush` to Hush Chain and raised three thresholds, ids unchanged), **525 points**, 475 held back; none hidden or repeatable; none rewards hinge speed or move count; several reward quiet (Hushed, the chain, no bites). | 22 | **App Privacy** | **Data Not Collected** (confirm with the grep in `privacy.md` §3 on the RC) | No network, SDKs or accounts by design; the hinge reading never leaves the phone; Game Center data is Apple's to disclose |
| 23 | **Accessibility labels** | Claim **Dark Interface**, **Sufficient Contrast**, **Differentiate Without Color Alone**, **Reduced Motion**, each only if its check passes; not VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text | Hush is dark by design and every tell is on screen and in sound and haptics; play needs sight of the lit loot and dragging |

## 3. Decisions for the founder to sanity-check

1. **Launch Tue 1 Dec**, manual release at 08:00 UK, nomination **by Tue 10 Nov**, fallback Tue 15 Dec.
2. **Age rating 9+** rather than 4+ (the comic bite is mild cartoon peril).
3. **Categories Casual + Adventure** rather than Puzzle.
4. **Trademark:** run the UK IPO and USPTO searches for "HUSH" (runbook §1a) before the record is created; switch to "Tiptoe" if a games mark is live.
5. **Store words:** subtitle `Open slowly. Steal the gold.` (or `The game where you're gentle`).
6. **Support through GitHub issues** and the copyright line copied from Squish. Say if you'd rather have a support email or a different copyright holder.
7. **No touch fallback on a Duo** (your Round 3 decision 4): App Review may ask how to play a Duo without the hinge. The notes point them to any other iPhone or the Simulator; say if you'd rather add a "Play with the lever" setting before submission.
8. **Achievement points** re-weighted for the R2 swaps (Midas Touch 25, Hush Chain 75; still 525 in total).
